    How to Play

    1. Customers walk up and ask for a number of items. Tap a customer to work on their order. Each customer has their own bag.
    2. Tap a food bin to fry it. When it glows golden, tap it to put it in the selected customer's bag. Tap burnt food to throw it away.
    3. Tap the sauce jar they asked for (and the palamig jug once unlocked), then press Hand over order.
    4. The customer pays with a bill or coins. Work out the total from the prices on the bins, tap bills and coins in the cash drawer to give the exact change, then press Give change.
    5. Fast service earns tips. Wait too long and customers leave. Earn pesos to level up, unlock new food, and open more counter spots.
    6. Reach Level 5 and earn ₱1000 to become a Master Vendor.
